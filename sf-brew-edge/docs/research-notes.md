# Research notes

- Staff POS pain in taprooms is speed and context switching during rush periods.
- One-tap issue signaling is safer than form-first workflows during service peaks.
- Keg/tap operations need low-fill visibility to avoid waste and 86 surprises.
- Promo automation without manager approval can damage floor operations.
- PCI-safe POS-adjacent architecture reduces risk by never entering card-data handling scope.

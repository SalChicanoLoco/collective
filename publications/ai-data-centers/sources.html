AI Data Centers in Arid Regions: evidence spine.

Centros de datos de IA en regiones áridas: columna de evidencia.

This page separates what the public articles say from the evidence structure behind them. It is a working review page, not the final bibliography.

Esta página separa lo que dicen los artículos públicos de la estructura de evidencia detrás de ellos. Es una página de revisión en proceso, no la bibliografía final.

Method

Claims need spines

Every strong claim should trace to a public source, calculation, assumption, or visible caveat.

Claim categories

What the package must support

Energy

Electric load growth

U.S. and global data-center energy demand estimates, projection ranges, and uncertainty bands.

Water

Cooling and consumption

Water usage effectiveness, cooling method, water source, basin stress, and evaporative versus closed-loop assumptions.

Infrastructure

Grid, land, and utility burden

Transmission, generation, wastewater, local infrastructure commitments, and public cost exposure.

Economy

Public leverage

Jobs claims, industrial revenue, tax incentives, public approvals, procurement, and community benefits.

Geography

Arid-region risk

Southwestern climate, drought exposure, groundwater and surface-water constraints, and rural governance context.

Policy

Disclosure standard

Why PUE is not enough without WUE, water source, cooling method, energy source, and local resource context.

Working source classes

Bibliography buckets

Federal / national reports

DOE, LBNL, IEA, EIA, and other institutional sources for energy, compute, and infrastructure baselines.

State and local records

County records, utility materials, IRB documents, economic-development packages, and public meeting records.

Technical standards

PUE, WUE, CUE, cooling-system definitions, and data-center efficiency/disclosure frameworks.

Watchdog / journalism

Credible reporting used only with date, attribution, and separation from primary-source claims.

Source-review checklist

For every factual claim

Add source name, publisher, publication date, access date, link, and which package page uses the claim.

For every number

Record unit, conversion path, assumption, uncertainty range, and whether the figure is measured, estimated, or projected.

For every quote

Keep excerpts short, preserve context, and prefer paraphrase plus citation for public pages.

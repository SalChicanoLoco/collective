Water scenario formula

Annual water use can be estimated from IT energy and WUE:

annual_liters = IT_energy_kWh * WUE_L_per_kWh
annual_gallons = annual_liters / 3.78541

Disclosure rule

A project should not report PUE without WUE in an arid basin. PUE alone can make water-intensive evaporative cooling look efficient while hiding the resource cost that matters most locally.

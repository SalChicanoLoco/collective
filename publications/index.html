Publication taxonomy

Every serious package gets the same spine.

1

Hub

Plain-language overview, status, audience routes, version history, and download links.

2

Public brief

Short readable article for general readers and social sharing.

3

Longform

Periodical-style version with deeper context, visuals, and source notes.

4

Policy / political brief

Actionable version for decision-makers, advocates, boards, and public comment.

5

Evidence spine

Sources, assumptions, calculations, definitions, caveats, and provenance.

6

Bilingual set

Reviewed English and Spanish pages, not mixed-mode copy or live unreviewed translation.

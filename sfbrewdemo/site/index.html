HTTPS

Deploy on Netlify or Cloudflare Pages for automatic TLS plus edge headers. GitHub Pages is acceptable for quick HTTPS demo hosting.

BrewOS

Do not replace BrewOS first. Inventory, back up, isolate, virtualize, and augment reporting outside payment-card scope.

Scope

No checkout, no cardholder data, no facial recognition, no customer tracking, no auto-publishing. Human approval stays in the loop.

1. The Core Dry-Bulb Temperature Morphing Formulation

To preserve diurnal and seasonal weather patterns while injecting long-term Global Climate Model (GCM) warming trends, a combined shift and stretch operation is applied:

Monthly Anomaly: ΔTm = Tm(GCM, future) − Tm(GCM, baseline) Variability Scaling Factor: αm = ΔTMAX,m − ΔTMIN,m ⟨Tmax⟩m,a − ⟨Tmin⟩m,a Future Hourly Dry-Bulb Temperature: Tf = Ta + ΔTm + αm ⋅ ( Ta − ⟨Tm,a⟩ )
LaTeX source 
\Delta T_m = T_m(\text{GCM, future}) - T_m(\text{GCM, baseline})
\alpha_m = \frac{\Delta T_{\text{MAX}, m} - \Delta T_{\text{MIN}, m}}{\langle T_{\text{max}} \rangle_{m, a} - \langle T_{\text{min}} \rangle_{m, a}}
T_f = T_a + \Delta T_m + \alpha_m \cdot \left(T_a - \langle T_{m, a} \rangle\right)

2. Spatial Interpolation (Inverse Distance Weighting)

Because GCM grids are coarse (~300 km for HadCM3, ~100 km for CMIP6), anomalies at site location (x, y) are weighted inversely by distance squared from the 4 surrounding nodes:

wi = 1di2 Anomaly(site) = ∑wi⋅Anomalyi ∑wi
LaTeX source 
w_i = \frac{1}{d_i^2}
\text{Anomaly}(\text{site}) = \frac{\sum w_i \cdot \text{Anomaly}_i}{\sum w_i}
